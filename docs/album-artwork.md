# Local album artwork

In Collection → Albums, open a named album and use the image button in its header to choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image, or remove the chosen image. The selected image is copied to Aura's private application-support directory and displayed in album lists, track lists, the queue and the in-app player. The original file, audio files and tags are not modified. Input is limited to 10 MiB and the stored copy is resized to at most 1024 pixels on its longest side.

For metadata-derived albums, matching follows the current library grouping: the exact album name identifies a group, so albums sharing a name also share a selected cover, regardless of artist or source. Personal albums have their own independent cover even if their names match another album; tracks within them display that cover during playback. Tracks without an album do not have a selectable metadata-album cover. Removing an override falls back to the existing artwork. Covers remain local to the device and are not sent to Navidrome. New Aura backups include them, subject to the documented backup size limit. Android system notifications may continue using the original artwork.

Automated tests cover persistence, replacing invalid input, interruption recovery, and refreshing multiple views. On Android, selection and removal were verified using a synthetic image on a local album. The Windows release was built and its startup verified; selecting a cover on Windows has not yet been manually tested.

On Android the cover menu also offers the system Photos picker. A cloud photo provider such as Google Photos may be available depending on Android and the provider configuration. Aura copies the selected image into its existing private cover store and enforces the same size and image validation. The system picker option has a widget test but has not yet been checked on a device or with a cloud photo.

## Online cover search

The cover menu can search MusicBrainz releases using an explicitly submitted, editable title and optional artist. The entered text is sent to MusicBrainz. Choosing a candidate explicitly requests its front thumbnail from Cover Art Archive and Internet Archive; these providers can see the device IP. Aura shows the image and release details for confirmation before replacing the local cover. Results may be wrong or unavailable; cancel leaves the existing cover unchanged. Requests to MusicBrainz share the rate limiter with track metadata search. Image redirects are restricted to the archive hosts and upgraded to HTTPS, and the response is limited to 10 MiB before existing image validation. Online searches have been tested with simulated responses, not a live service or a device. No audio files or Navidrome addresses are sent. Texts are Romanian and English, with English fallback in the other supported locales.
