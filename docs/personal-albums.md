# Personal albums

Open Collection → Albums and choose New album beside the album search field. Enter a name, select tracks (search can narrow the list), then save. Personal albums appear alongside metadata-derived albums and can be searched. Open an album to play its tracks with the in-app player and seek bar, choose or remove a cover, edit its name and membership, or delete the album. Deleting an album leaves audio files unchanged. The original media metadata and server libraries are not modified.

An album has its own ID and cover even when its name matches another album. Its name and track references are included in Aura backups and remapped on restoration; existing references to temporarily unavailable tracks survive edits. Selected cover images are stored privately and included in new Aura backups, subject to the documented backup size limit. Album covers shown by Android system notifications may remain the original artwork.

Tests use synthetic data and cover saved-state persistence and failures, backup remapping, rename migration, queue cover metadata, search and independent identities, selection through search, editing with unavailable tracks, and safe deletion. On Android a temporary album was created from a local AUD file, retained after restart, given a synthetic cover, and deleted; the original track remained in its library. The final Android build displayed the mini-player and seek slider inside an album, and seeking was verified. The Windows build was compiled and its previous version was startup-tested; creation has not been manually verified on Windows.
